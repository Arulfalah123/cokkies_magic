Baked The Slow Way

01

Mix by hand

Stone milled flour, cultured butter and raw cane sugar folded together in small batches, never by machine alone.

02

Rest overnight

Every dough sleeps for at least twelve hours in the cold room, so the flavour has time to deepen before it meets heat.

03

Bake at dawn

Ovens fire up at four in the morning. Whatever lands on the shelf was baked only a few hours before you taste it.
